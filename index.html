How to use
• 🧮 CALCULATOR — numbers aur operators se calculate karo
• 🔒 STICKY OP — operator ko long-press karo, baar baar same op use karo. Long-press phir se → exit
• 🗓️ DATE CALC — heading pe tap karo, date diff nikaalo
• 👥 SPLITTER — heading pe tap karo, bill split karo
• ⚙️ SETTINGS — theme badlo aur Soft Press band karo
• 🏷️ LABEL — kisi digit pe tap karo, usme label add karo
• 💸 EXPENSES — amount label karo, phir labeled digit pe dubara tap karo to record ho jayega. Settings mein on/off karo
• 📜 HISTORY — purane calculations dekho aur reuse karo
• 📤 SHARE — WhatsApp pe result bhejo
